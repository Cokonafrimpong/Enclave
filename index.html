Ghana's First
Coastal Community

Take a departure and discover an entirely new beachfront lifestyle outside Accra. The Enclave defines Ghana's first master-planned coastal community — a mixed-use live + work + play development set across 267 acres along the Atlantic Ocean and Dawa Lagoon.

Explore Masterplan → View Investment Vision

Our Vision

To create a world-class coastal destination that establishes a benchmark for design, sustainability, functionality, and urban living — a place where people desire to live, work, recreate, and relax.

Smart Waterfront Living

A connected waterfront environment that integrates residential, hospitality, recreation, and commercial experiences.

Sustainable Growth

Development principles that prioritize environmental stewardship, green infrastructure, and responsible use of natural resources.

Walkable Neighbourhoods

Designed to encourage walking, cycling, recreation, and vibrant public interaction throughout every district.

Designed For The Way
People Want To Live.

The Enclave is envisioned as a cohesive planned community where architecture, landscape, infrastructure, and public spaces work together to create an exceptional living environment. The development combines modern coastal living with carefully preserved natural assets, including the Dawa Lagoon, wetlands, beaches, and green spaces.

  • ✓
