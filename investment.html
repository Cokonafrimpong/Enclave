Why The Enclave?

Six converging factors make The Enclave one of the most compelling investment positions in West Africa.

Location

Located 25 miles from Accra on the Gulf of Guinea coast, The Enclave encompasses the Dawa Lagoon which connects directly to the Atlantic Ocean — making these residences an ultimate primary sanctuary within easy reach of the city.

Abidjan–Lagos Coastal Stretch

Strategically located along the 600-mile coastal stretch from Abidjan to Lagos — described by The Guardian as the world's most rapidly growing urbanising region, projected to reach 51 million people by 2035, driving commerce along the entire route.

Investment Returns

The project is projected to generate an unlevered Net Profit of $22.2M. Levered Net Profit, assuming a 3-year debt facility at 40% Loan-to-Cost and pricing at 10%, is expected to reach $14.62M.

Demand for High-Quality Accommodation

With Ghana emerging as the African destination of choice for the diaspora, high-income professionals, and high-net-worth investors, The Enclave meets the moment — providing high-end ocean getaway and luxury residences for primary and secondary uses.

Increasing Population

Accra's population is projected to surpass 10 million by 2030. This growth will intensify demand for quality housing, making serene ocean-front communities the new frontier for single-family residential development.

Sustainability

The Enclave incorporates UN Sustainable Development Goals, IFC Standards, and ESG benchmarks — creating direct positive impact through job creation, wildlife-friendly green spaces, mental well-being, and a blueprint for sustainable coastal development in West Africa.

Enter Early. Grow Long.

Five phases of development create distinct entry points at different stages of the project lifecycle.

Phase One
2025–2027
Land & Infrastructure

Ground-floor land acquisition. Site works, primary roads, utility installation, and first residential plots released. Earliest entry pricing available at this stage.

Phase Two
2027–2029
Residential Expansion

Second residential release with growing infrastructure visibility. Rising asset values as community amenities come online.

Phase Three
2029–2031
Waterfront & Commercial

Waterfront commercial lots, marina development, and mixed-use zones. Active community with promenade and retail taking shape.

Phase Four
2031–2033
Hospitality & Resort

Resort, hotel, and high-density residential zones. Hospitality infrastructure opens the community to wider visitors and investors.

Phase Five
2033–2035
Completion

Full community operational with all amenities, green spaces, and the golf park corridor. Completed-asset value reflecting a fully functioning coastal destination.
