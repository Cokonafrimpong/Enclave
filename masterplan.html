A Blueprint for
Coastal Community Living

267 acres of prime beachfront and hilly ocean-side land on the Gulf of Guinea coast in Nyanyano, Gomoa Fetteh — developed into 396 mixed-use serviced plots for sale across five phases. The site is approximately 75 minutes from Accra, accessible by the N1 highway from Accra to Cape Coast.

The land contains a lagoon, with a 1-mile shoreline and approximately 1.2 miles of ocean frontage. Upon completion, the 267 acres will comprise 169 acres of serviced plots, 50 acres of road, 46 acres of green spaces, and 2 acres of beachfront walkway — alongside a future 18-hole golf park and well-designed communal infrastructure including streets, lighting, drainage, cycle lanes, and pavements.

The Plan, Layer by Layer

Each planning layer reveals how the site is organised, from green corridors and water features to roads, pedestrian paths, and zone designations.

Green Space

Green & Ecological Corridors

27% of The Enclave's land area is dedicated to parks and gardens — designed to encourage exercise, social activities, and foster well-being within the community. Coupled with the Dawa Lagoon on-site and a 1.2-mile beach shoreline, the development is positioned to become one of the most desirable and well-designed communities on the coast of West Africa.

  • ● 27% of land area dedicated to parks & gardens
  • ● Dawa Lagoon integrated as ecological & community asset
  • ● Reduces heat-island effect, water run-off & noise pollution
  • ● Supports physical health, community growth & crime prevention

Waterways & Coastal Edge

Due to the hilly terrain across parts of the site, the masterplan incorporates water retention ponds to ease excess flow of water into the lagoon and ocean, mitigating stormwater run-off. The retention ponds surround the base areas of the hills, with release ducts connecting directly to the Dawa Lagoon and the Atlantic Ocean — turning a natural drainage challenge into an engineered landscape asset.

  • ● Water retention ponds at base of hilly terrain areas
  • ● Release ducts drain to Dawa Lagoon & Atlantic Ocean
  • ● Stormwater run-off mitigated across entire site
  • ● 1.2-mile beach shoreline on the Atlantic coast

Road Network & Entry Points

A hierarchical road network separates regional through-traffic from residential circulation. Two controlled entry points manage community access. A primary spine road connects the main gate to the waterfront, with internal loops serving each district.

  • ● 2 controlled primary entry gatehouses
  • ● Primary spine boulevard: gate to waterfront
  • ● Hierarchy: primary, secondary & residential lanes
  • ● Through-traffic fully separated from residential roads

Pedestrian & Cycling Network

The masterplan prioritises soft mobility over vehicular traffic. Electric bicycles, golf carts, and concierge shuttling are the recommended primary modes of movement within the community — reducing vehicular pollution and creating a quieter, more liveable environment. Local two-lane roads connect all zoning districts, while the four-lane spine road handles peripheral boundary access. Pedestrian destinations include a central park zone, beachfront promenade, and a farmers' market area.

  • ● E-bike and golf cart recommended as primary internal transport
  • ● Multiple e-bike rental shops within the community
  • ● Golf cart shuttling services for all residents and visitors
  • ● Central park zone, promenade & farmers' market as key pedestrian destinations

Land Use Zones

Five clearly defined zones create a coherent mixed-use community, each with a distinct character, density, and purpose, all woven into a single integrated plan.

R1

Single Family District

354 plots · 69.60 acres. Low-density single-family homes. Permitted: single family dwellings, neighbourhood parks. Conditional: duplex, day/child care, community gardens.

R2

Multi-Family District — Low Density

30 plots · 12.86 acres. Single-, two-, and three-family dwellings and cluster developments. Permitted: duplexes, neighbourhood parks, community gardens.

R5

Multi-Family District — High Density

4 plots · 67.60 acres. High-density apartments and congregate living, minimum 5,000 sq.ft. lots. Permitted: multi-family (3+ units), hotels, single-room occupancy housing.

OR1

Mixed-Use — Office & Residence

7 plots · 14.27 acres. Mixed-use residential, office, institutional, and small-scale retail. Permitted: medical facilities, offices, educational facilities, neighbourhood retail (cafes, grocery, hardware).

C1

Neighbourhood Commercial

1 plot · 4.50 acres. Small-scale retail and commercial services. Permitted: retail, offices, medical facilities, financial institutions, EV charging. Conditional: wine bars, night establishments.

Built to Last

Connected, resilient infrastructure underpins every aspect of the masterplan, from roads to utilities to digital connectivity.

Roads & Circulation

A hierarchical road network separating vehicular and pedestrian movement, with dedicated cycling lanes, clear signage, and logical entry and exit control points.

Connected Utilities

Reliable electricity, potable water, wastewater treatment, stormwater drainage, and digital fibre infrastructure designed for long-term community performance.

Security & Access Control

Gated access points, perimeter security, CCTV coverage, and access management systems designed to deliver safety without sacrificing openness in public zones.

Solar Energy Farm

A dedicated 5-acre solar farm with two sub-stations provides renewable energy infrastructure for the entire community — powering The Enclave sustainably for generations.
