Where the Community
Meets the Ocean

The waterfront district is the social heart of The Enclave. A continuous public promenade runs along the Atlantic beachfront, lined with shade structures, seating, and access points down to the sand.

Marina facilities serve small craft and leisure boats, while a curated waterfront retail and dining strip provides residents and visitors with everyday conveniences alongside memorable experiences.

  • ✓
    Atlantic Beach Promenade

    A continuous public walkway along the coast, accessible to all residents.

  • ✓
    Marina & Boat Access

    Dedicated facilities for leisure watercraft and coastal recreation.

  • ✓
    Waterfront Retail & Dining

    Curated food, beverage, and lifestyle outlets within a pedestrian-friendly strip.

Private Living,
Connected Community

Each residential enclave within The Enclave is designed as a self-contained neighbourhood — with controlled access, landscaped streets, play areas, and communal garden space — while remaining connected to the wider community via an extensive pedestrian network.

  • ✓
    Gated Access & Security

    Manned entry points and perimeter systems for residential privacy.

  • ✓
    Clean Modern Architecture

    Simple, bold contemporary design with coastal-sensitive materials and clean lines.

  • ✓
    Structured Parking & Circulation

    Dedicated parking zones that free the street-level for pedestrians and greenery.

Simple, Modern,
Coastal.

The architectural direction for The Enclave draws on the principles of simplicity, sensitivity to the coastal environment, and contemporary relevance. Facades are clean and bold. Materials are durable and low-maintenance. Colours are drawn from the natural palette of sand, sea, and sky.

Public spaces are generously landscaped with shade trees, ground cover, and coastal-hardy planting that thrives in the Gomoa Fetteh climate. Every street, path, and open space is designed to feel inviting — not merely functional.
