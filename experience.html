Life at
The Enclave

267 acres between the Atlantic Ocean and the Dawa Lagoon — waterfront living, walkable neighbourhoods, and a lifestyle unlike anything else in West Africa.

Water

Live by
the Water

Positioned between the Atlantic coastline and the Dawa Lagoon, The Enclave offers a waterfront lifestyle at every turn.

  • ✓
    Waterfront Promenade

    A continuous public walkway along the Atlantic beachfront, open to all residents.

  • ✓
    Marina & Boating

    Lagoon and ocean access for leisure watercraft, fishing, and coastal recreation.

  • ✓
    Waterfront Cafés & Dining

    Restaurants and gathering spaces along the water's edge at sunset.

  • ✓
    Boardwalks & Scenic Overlooks

    Lagoon-side paths and viewpoints integrated into the masterplan.

  • ✓
    Jogging & Walking Trails

    Shaded, landscaped routes connecting neighbourhoods, parks, and the beach.

  • ✓
    E-Bike & Golf Cart Mobility

  • ✓
    Car-Free Zones

Sustainability

Designed
Around Nature

Built to coexist with the coast, not override it.

Coastal & Lagoon Protection

Lagoon preservation areas, ecological buffers, protected wetlands, and conservation-focused planning safeguard the Dawa Lagoon and Atlantic edge.

Green Infrastructure

Tree-lined streets, native planting, green corridors, and open-space networks improve environmental performance across the entire site.

Water-Sensitive Design

Retention ponds, natural drainage corridors, wetland systems, and stormwater infrastructure protect the ecosystem and manage hilly terrain run-off.

Smart Infrastructure

Reliable utilities, community-wide connectivity, efficient services, and future-ready systems built for long-term resilience and high quality of life.

Solar Energy Farm

A dedicated 5-acre solar farm with two sub-stations is planned to power the entire community through renewable energy — a commitment to energy resilience and a sustainable future for coastal living in Ghana.

Low-Carbon Mobility

E-bikes, golf carts, and a pedestrian-first street network reduce car dependency throughout the community — connecting every district cleanly and quietly.

For Every Generation

A Destination
for Every Stage of Life

Families, professionals, retirees, and visitors — The Enclave is planned to serve people at every stage of life, from playgrounds and open lawns to waterfront dining, wellness spaces, and hospitality destinations.
